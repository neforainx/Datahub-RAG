# Project Lark — Team Journal (SAMPLE corpus)

Fictional sample content so a fresh install can be tried end to end.
Replace the whole corpus folder with your own notes.

## Decisions

### Refund policy

Decided 14 October 2026: customers can request a full refund within
14 days of purchase, no questions asked. After 14 days, refunds are
case-by-case and need a team lead's approval. Refunds are paid back to
the original payment method within 5 business days.

### Weekly sync

The weekly sync is every Tuesday at 09:30. Agenda: last week's
deliverables, blockers, and the plan for the week. Notes from each
sync go into this journal.

## Operations

### Deploy freeze

No deploys on Fridays after 15:00 and during the last two days of
each month. Hotfixes for payment failures are the only exception.

### Vendor choice

We picked Northwind as the email vendor on 21 October 2026 because
its per-message pricing beat the alternatives at our volume and its
delivery reports can be exported as CSV.
